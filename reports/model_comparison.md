### Draw rule

Plain argmax never predicts a draw, because a draw is almost never the single most likely outcome (the bookmaker's prices never make it one either). The draw rule predicts a draw when the draw probability reaches a threshold chosen on the walk-forward seasons to maximise macro F1. Probabilities, log loss and the betting simulation are unchanged.
